# TraceWarrant completion boundary

The agreed product is an experimental, open-source Python library/CLI for
observation conformance, simulator-neutral frozen prediction validation, cheap
baseline comparison and portable quality/performance evidence. The separately
authorized V10 increment adds usable bounded observation collection and a pinned
framework integration. It helps test performance claims and report UNKNOWN or
negative results. It is not a standalone capacity planner or certification service.

**Current state: all agreed engineering/release stages are complete. Required
remaining stages: 0.** Latest version: **0.8.1**, an experimental release label.
Completion is 100% of the defined scope, not a promise to reach version 1.0 or
evidence of production readiness, external adoption or scientific superiority.
No further stage is queued.

The original ten V3 criteria and finite V4–V9 workflows are mapped to preserved
evidence in [V9 acceptance](docs/v9/acceptance.md), its
[machine-readable record](docs/v9/acceptance.json) and
[verified 0.7.1 closeout](docs/v9/public-release-0.7.1.md). The maintainer then
requested concrete next-phase research on 2026-10-14. All four new
[V10 gates](PROJECT_V10.md) are complete: researched need, neutral recorder,
framework integration and installed/hosted/measured/public qualification.
[Verified 0.8.0 closeout](docs/v10/public-release-0.8.0.md),
[V10 acceptance](docs/v10/acceptance.json). Prior acceptance and tags stay immutable.

The collector's actual boundaries, opaque IDs, bounded content-free output and
optional export reduce duplicated collection code. Native framework coverage
remains 2/4 and its full graph/GPU demand unsupported; short-operation recording
cost is material and published. Caller declarations are not authenticated evidence.

FRAMES showed no added intervention decision value beyond utilization; HotpotQA
failed its quality gate. Preserve those results. More RAM or successful TestModel
integration does not unlock the failed study or establish useful-agent capacity.
No model rerun, private answer publication, paid/cloud work, PyPI upload or outreach
is required for closure. Future maintenance, independently motivated experiments,
external adoption or production requirements are new scope, not unfinished stages.
