# Evidence-gated roadmap

## Final V9 acceptance — one remaining stage

[V9](PROJECT_V9.md) audits the original requirements, closes a reproduced
performance-HTML verification gap and qualifies patch 0.7.1. Its four finite
gates are the final required work for this scope. [Acceptance map](docs/v9/acceptance.md),
[finish line](PROJECT_COMPLETION.md). No further feature stage is scheduled.

## Completed V8 portable quality evidence

[V8](PROJECT_V8.md) fills the quality evidence sharing gap: native bounded export
and offline checksum/JSON/HTML replay. All four scoped stages passed: 288 tests
per matrix job, installed/hosted qualification and experimental 0.7.0 public
download/source-free replay/refusal verification. [Guide](docs/v8/quality-bundles.md),
[closeout](docs/v8/public-release-0.7.0.md). No additional V8 gate is queued.

## Completed V7 declared-population and policy binding

[V7](PROJECT_V7.md) addresses the next explicit workflow gap: enforce an exact
quality policy and declared population against a separately frozen receipt.
Optional bound qualification preserves the existing unbound interface and
refuses dropped/substituted tasks or changed policy/plan bytes. Documentation,
installed/hosted checks and experimental 0.6.0 publication are complete: 260 tests
per hosted matrix job, ten public assets, fresh bound replay and omission refusal.
[Closeout](docs/v7/public-release-0.6.0.md). No additional stage is queued.

## Completed V6 reusable quality prerequisite

[V6](PROJECT_V6.md) addresses a concrete V5 integration gap: completion, exact
fraction and mean answer F1 need a standalone quality decision before any
performance prediction. Strict schemas, offered-population multiple gates,
CLI/reports, retained-data adapter and installed/public-release qualification are
complete. [0.5.0 closeout](docs/v6/public-release-0.5.0.md): 244 tests per hosted
matrix job, ten public assets and fresh source-free replay. It preserves V5 and
does not resume its locked performance study. No additional stage is queued.

## Completed V5 application qualification

The maintainer requested the next real-application stage on 2026-10-14.
[V5](PROJECT_V5.md) executed one public HotpotQA local QA application, with
official answer scoring and fixed disjoint samples. Eight requests completed,
4/8 exact answers and mean answer F1 0.541667 failed its predeclared quality gate.
Performance-model and cheap-baseline comparison were not unlocked. The negative
study, toolkit and 0.4.0 release are closed and preserved. [Results](docs/v5/results.md).

This is not unfinished work for the completed experimental release.
