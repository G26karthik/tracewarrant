# ADR-0001 Project thesis

Status: ACCEPTED

Date: 2026-09-28

## Context
The broad digital-twin idea overlaps with AgentServeSim, AISimulate and PerfSim.
## Requirements
One systems contribution, testable value, modest hardware and open evidence.
## Alternatives considered
Full runtime; inference planner; upstream adapter; heterogeneous contention research.
## Decision
Investigate calibrated non-LLM contention and real intervention validation as a conditional thesis.
## Why
A controllable tool pool can alter deployment choices independently of inference. This is a hypothesis.
## Consequences
M1 is analytical infrastructure, not proof of predictive value.
## Risks
Calibration can cost more than direct testing; competitors can subsume the scope.
## Evidence
[Landscape](../research/competitive-landscape.md), [adversarial review](../research/adversarial-review.md).
## Reversal conditions
If two bounded calibration iterations cannot improve held-out decisions over simple baselines, narrow to an integration/validation library.
