# Preregistered prediction criteria — controlled pilot 1

Date: 2026-09-29. Written before any final holdout execution. M1.5 captures and timer diagnostics are exploratory, not held-out evaluation. The complete criteria and frozen model/prediction file must be committed before holdout collection. No thresholds may be changed after looking at those measurements; failed/ambiguous cells stay in the report.

This is a controlled non-coding workload with stub inference and real asyncio queues, SQLite and local retrieval/tool functions. It can validate the measurement/model/intervention pipeline but **cannot validate the full thesis for meaningful model-driven agents**. An external PydanticAI TestModel path has been compatibility-tested separately. No task-quality or real inference claim will be made.

Workload v2 scales controlled delays by 10: planning/synthesis≈30 ms each, retrieval≈60 ms, tool≈150 ms, SQLite hold≈20 ms, external wait≈50 ms. The previous short-delay workload is unsuitable for calibration on a 15.625 ms Windows monotonic clock; preserve its observations. Use standard event loop, record actual timestamps rather than nominal sleeps. This design change occurs before calibration/holdout, not after a bad confirmatory result.

Calibration: BASELINE pools tool=1, stub-model=2, retrieval=2, database=1. Loads 2 and 9 offered sessions/s, two sequential repetitions of 40 sessions each, no intentional failure injection. Distinct time batches; no held-out spans fit parameters. Fit per-node empirical occupied/external distributions, compare means with exponential/CV assumptions, report tails and within-session correlations. Whole-session bootstrap is the uncertainty unit. No automatic exponential fit. Record application/source, hardware, clock, configuration and raw input digest. If service behavior varies materially by load, do not silently merge: retain load cohorts, choose nearest training load for each prediction and report that interpolation assumption.

Confirmatory holdout: offered rates 6 and 8 sessions/s, BASELINE / TOOL+ (2 tool workers) / MODEL+ (4 model workers) / RETRIEVAL+ (4 retrieval workers). Two fresh repetitions of 60 sessions per cell. Fixed task distribution and open arrivals, all sessions drained; report offered count, actual arrivals, completions, errors, elapsed window, p50/p95/p99, queue and occupancy per pool. All cells are attempted once the predictions are frozen. A runtime failure stops the affected sweep with its partial results preserved; it does not delete unfavorable cells.

Freeze predictions for every cell using 30 simulator seeds (1000–1029), same explicit planned arrival schedule and 60 sessions per run. Primary model uses per-node empirical samples; a paired-session resampling sensitivity is reported if supported before freeze. Report median predicted quantiles and seed ranges, not an invented 95% prediction interval. Independent seed variation only captures scenario variability, not model discrepancy. Observed bootstrap intervals concern this fixed workload/session sample and remain vulnerable to temporal correlation.

Acceptance (unchanged after holdout):

- Median p95 relative error across all eight supported cells <=15%. Also publish every cell's signed/absolute/relative error, p50/p99 and replication spread. Low-count p99 is explicitly underpowered.
- Correct intervention ordering >=80% of materially separated intervention pairs. A pair is material only if both observed repetitions agree in direction and pooled p95 differs by >=10%. Report all ties/ambiguous pairs and denominator; zero eligible pairs is insufficient evidence.
- First contended pool agrees in a majority of clear BASELINE cells. Clear means highest mean queue component >=2 times the runner-up and at least 5 ms/session. Other cells are ambiguous.
- Saturation is not identifiable from a short draining finite batch as a steady-state threshold. No saturation-accuracy pass or sustainable-capacity claim from this pilot. A future long stationary sweep is required for the <=15% saturation criterion.
- Compare inference-only planning (chooses MODEL+), fixed tool delay (no independent tool queue), naive scaling (total slot increase), utilization heuristic (largest offered occupied demand / configured slots), direct load testing and the full model. If the utilization heuristic matches every material decision, record lack of added decision value and reassess product scope. Simulator agreement alone does not permit optimization.

No failed/censored records may be discarded to improve fit; the narrow fitter must refuse incomplete/failing cohorts or report unsupported models. No pooling across app/hardware/pool versions. No retries/branch probability, outages, GPU capacity or quality inferred from this no-failure pilot. Extrapolation beyond the declared loads/configurations is unsupported. Model and measured service invariance under interventions is a hypothesis being tested, not guaranteed by provenance labels.
